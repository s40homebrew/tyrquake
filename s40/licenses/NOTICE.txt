TyrQuake (libretro fork), based on Quake by id Software.
Source: https://github.com/libretro/tyrquake
Base commit: 9dcc3ff4ccf96d2f7aa029b738f2c90685b9257f (2024-12-27).
Engine and RM-839 port: GPL-2.0-or-later; see COPYING.QUAKE and COPYING.RM839.
The native bootstrap/runtime is adapted from the S40 Doom and ClassiCube ports. Shared foundation MIT notice: COPYING.S40.
Libretro API header MIT notices are retained in COPYING.LIBRETRO.
Newlib/libm and libgcc are from Arm GNU Toolchain 15.3.rel2 soft-float archives.
See COPYING.NEWLIB, COPYING.GCC3 and COPYING.RUNTIME (GCC runtime exception).
No Quake game data or complete Nokia firmware is included in this package.
Addresses and short identity fingerprints bind to RM-839 firmware 02.33.
